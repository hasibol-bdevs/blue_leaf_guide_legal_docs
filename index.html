This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how Blue Leaf Guide (“Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects your information when you access or use our cosmetology education mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information Provided by You

  • Full name and Email address

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and secure your account, track educational progress, manage billing, and comply with legal responsibilities. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary for service continuity or legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

We share information only with trusted partners necessary for app operation, such as payment processors (Stripe, Apple/Google) and cloud hosting providers.

We implement technical and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no system is 100% secure.
